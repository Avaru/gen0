# Sovereignty name sources and limits

Checked 2026-10-10. Editable entries and relative weights live in `src/data/sovereignties.ts`; personal names continue to come from the existing sourced nationality lists. No name lists were copied from lore pages.

## Research basis

The publisher's [Eurosource Plus description](https://talsorianstore.com/collections/cyberpunk/products/eurosource-plus) establishes the European Community as a major Cyberpunk setting region. [CD Projekt's Phantom Liberty page](https://www.cyberpunk.net/pt/en/phantom-liberty) establishes the NUSA / Night City context. Public [Native American Nations Volume One excerpts](https://d1vzi28wh99zvq.cloudfront.net/pdf_previews/145975-sample.pdf) identify Shadowrun's Salish-Shidhe, Sioux and Pueblo states. Community lore indexes supply the broader political names and their sourcebook references: [Shadowrun countries](https://www.shadowrunwiki.com/Countries), [Shadowrun China](https://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/China), and [Cyberpunk Earth](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Earth). This is a research-backed selection, not a complete sourcebook audit.

The setting geography and names are lore facts. The selection of diaspora name pools and all relative weights are our practical inference for a varied generator. They are not canon census claims. A weight applies to the chance of choosing a naming tradition, independent of the number of names in its underlying list. Empty traditions are omitted and remaining weights are used proportionally.

## Compact choices

Shadowrun has 15 choices; Cyberpunk 2020/2077 has 8. Europe, Japan and India are shared entries. Ordinary nationality choices remain available separately and are placed last in the picker.

Each entry also carries its direct lore URL in the data file. Shared entries have additional setting references above. Latin spelling, gender forms, full Tamil father names, surname order and existing extra-name rules are preserved from the nationality generators.

## TODO handling

The 13 empty pools are Irish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Mexican, Brazilian Portuguese, Filipino, Ghanaian, Salish, Lakota, Dakota, Pueblo and Navajo. Selecting one directly shows a clear empty-pool message. Selecting a sovereignty with missing pools shows their labels beside the settings. Entirely empty Asamando remains selectable and explains that Ghanaian names are pending.

These labels reserve future work. Broad labels such as Ghanaian, Filipino, Salish and Pueblo require more specific sourced traditions and composition choices when filled. No borrowed general Native American names, fantasy syllables or guessed translations fill those gaps.
