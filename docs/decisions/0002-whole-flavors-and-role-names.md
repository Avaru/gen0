# 0002: Whole flavors and alternate role names

Date: 2026-10-10
Status: Accepted

Use three choices: Generic cyberpunk, Shadowrun, and Cyberpunk 2020/2077. Birka is postponed as described in decision 0023. Shadowrun includes species, dragons, magic, and its other setting details together. There are no feature checkboxes. This replaces the individual controls in decision 0001.

Shared concepts use stable IDs with default names and optional full/short flavor names. Netrunner becomes Decker in Shadowrun; Street Samurai has the short form Sam. Job Contact becomes Johnson in Shadowrun and Fixer in Cyberpunk 2020/2077. A Johnson generally represents the employer while a fixer brokers work; the shared concept is the crew's contact for a job, not an exact equivalence in lore.

Alternate spelling does not control availability. Technomancers and other identified magical entries carry the Shadowrun tag. Human age rules remain in generic generation, but species labels appear only with Shadowrun. Two duplicate role spellings were merged into their shared entries.

Data remains local TypeScript. Generation options pass through nested calls, and loading resolves names without changing the original lists. Programmatic include/exclude tags remain available; exclusions win.
